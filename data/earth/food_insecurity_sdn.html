Sudan — Acute Food Insecurity

IPC's analysis of Sudan, February 2026 - January 2027, in seven steps.

Scroll down, one step at a time.

1.What this map shows

These maps show IPC's analysis of acute food insecurity in Sudan, area by area, for three periods from February 2026 to January 2027.

IPC places each area in one of five phases, each with its color: Phase 1 Minimal, light green; Phase 2 Stressed, yellow; Phase 3 Crisis, orange; Phase 4 Emergency, red; Phase 5 Famine, dark red. White is an area IPC did not analyze for that period.

IPC's key for the map: "mapped Phase represents highest severity affecting at least 20% of the population." So an area's color can hide people in a worse phase. Step 5 shows where.

Phase 5 has two names in IPC's report: an area is classified Phase 5 (Famine), and people are classified Phase 5 (Catastrophe).

2.February - May 2026

IPC names this period Current. Its analysis was conducted in March - April 2026 and covered all of Sudan, 47.5 million people.

Nearly 19.5 million people were in Phase 3 or above (Crisis or worse), including over 5 million in Phase 4 (Emergency).

No area was classified Phase 5 (Famine), but nearly 135,000 people were in Phase 5 (Catastrophe).

The small circles are camps for internally displaced people (IDP settlements), in their phase color.

3.June - September 2026, the lean season

IPC names this period Projected 1. It covers 56 of Sudan's 195 areas, nearly 8.3 million of its 47.5 million people. The other areas are white. IPC: "A full nationwide projection analysis was not possible due to data gaps."

In those 56 areas, IPC projected 5.6 million people, 67 percent of the people analyzed, in Phase 3 or above: around 2.2 million in Phase 4 (Emergency), a further 3.1 million in Phase 3 (Crisis), and nearly 200,000 in Phase 5 (Catastrophe).

Comparing the same 56 areas with February - May, IPC notes the share in Phase 3 or above "rising from 60 percent to 67 percent."

IPC: "Caution is therefore advised when comparing figures across analysis periods, as geographic coverage differs significantly."

4.October 2026 - January 2027, the harvest season

This is the period under way now.

IPC names this period Projected 2. In the same 56 areas, IPC expects an estimated 4.9 million people, 59 percent of the people analyzed, to remain in Phase 3 or above: over 71,000 in Phase 5 (Catastrophe), 1.7 million in Phase 4 (Emergency) and 3 million in Phase 3 (Crisis).

IPC: from June - September, this share "is expected to decline to 59 percent."

The dashed box marks Darfur and Kordofan, shown closer in the next step.

5.Hidden under the colors

Closer in: the states of Darfur and Kordofan, October 2026 - January 2027.

An area takes the color of the highest phase affecting at least 20% of its people. Where fewer people than that are in Phase 5 (Catastrophe), the color does not show them.

Each dark red dot marks the people IPC counts in Phase 5 (Catastrophe) in one area; a larger dot means more people.

Across the 56 areas, IPC counts "over 71,000 people in Catastrophe (IPC Phase 5)" in this period.

6.Risk of Famine

IPC identifies a risk of Famine in 13 areas and IDP settlements in this period. Each carries IPC's symbol, a small dark red triangle with a white X.

IPC identifies them "under a reasonable worst-case scenario characterised by intensified conflict and further restrictions on humanitarian access and the movement of goods and people."

A risk of Famine is not a classification of Famine: IPC classifies no area Phase 5 (Famine) in this period.

7.About this map

From IPC: the data, the phase colors, the Risk of Famine symbol, the figures and the words.

This map adds:

  • Dark red dots for people in Phase 5 (Catastrophe), who can be hidden under a lower phase's color.
  • The three periods side by side, one folder each.
  • Google Earth's satellite globe beneath them.

It does not show:

  • IPC's food-assistance symbols.
  • The evidence levels for the two projections.
  • Acute malnutrition, which IPC classifies in its own separate analysis.
