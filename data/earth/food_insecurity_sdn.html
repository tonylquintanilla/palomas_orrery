Sudan — Acute Food Insecurity

IPC's analysis of Sudan, three periods, on Google Earth

Open the map in Google Earth

It opens in a new tab, on Projected 2: October 2026 - January 2027. Switch a period's folder on to see it.

Three periods

  • Current: February - May 2026
  • Projected 1: June - September 2026
  • Projected 2: October 2026 - January 2027

IPC's projection covers 56 areas out of 195 units of analysis of Sudan, equivalent to nearly 8.3 million people out of the total population of Sudan (47.5 million). Its numbers describe those 56 areas, not all of Sudan.

IPC: "Caution is therefore advised when comparing figures across analysis periods, as geographic coverage differs significantly."

What the colors mean

  • Phase 1 Minimal: light green
  • Phase 2 Stressed: yellow
  • Phase 3 Crisis: orange
  • Phase 4 Emergency: red
  • Phase 5 Famine / Catastrophe: dark red
  • Not analyzed: white
  • Risk of Famine (symbol after IPC): a small dark red triangle with a white X, on each area IPC lists at Risk of Famine (reasonable worst-case scenario)
  • Small circles: IDP settlements (camps for internally displaced people), in their phase color
  • Dark red dots: people in Phase 5 (Catastrophe); a larger dot means more people

The mapped phase is the highest severity affecting at least 20% of an area's population. A smaller Catastrophe population can sit under a Phase 4 color -- the full breakdown in each pop-up shows it.

How this map relates to IPC's

From IPC: the data, the phase colors, the Risk of Famine symbol, the figures and the words.

This map adds:

  • Dark red dots for people in Phase 5 (Catastrophe), who can be hidden under a lower phase's color.
  • The three periods side by side, one folder each.
  • Google Earth's satellite globe beneath them.

It does not show:

  • IPC's food-assistance symbols.
  • The evidence levels for the two projections.
  • Acute malnutrition, which IPC classifies in its own separate analysis.
