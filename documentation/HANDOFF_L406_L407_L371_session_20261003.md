**Sources read this session (Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-10-02/03):**

- `TERMINATION_SHOCK_AU`: Stone et al. 2005, Science 309, 2017,
  abstract (Caltech authors resolver) -- 94.01 AU. Store 94.01, 4
  figures. The orrery types "94 AU" in hovers and comments.
- `HELIOPAUSE_RADII` (26,148): Gurnett et al. 2013, Science 341, 1489,
  paper text -- 121 AU. Store a new `HELIOPAUSE_AU` = 121, 3 figures;
  `HELIOPAUSE_RADII` becomes derived from it (about 26,000), keeping
  its name for its ~60 consumers (L-386).
- `INNER_LIMIT_OORT_CLOUD_AU` (2,000) and `OUTER_OORT_CLOUD_AU`
  (100,000): NASA science.nasa.gov Oort Cloud facts -- inner edge
  between 2,000 and 5,000 AU, outer edge between 10,000 and 100,000
  AU. One figure each. The cited Hills (1981) and Oort (1950) do not
  print these numbers (Hills' abstract read).
- `INNER_OORT_CLOUD_AU` (20,000): Duncan, Quinn and Tremaine 1987, AJ
  94, 1330 -- the Hills cloud a < 20,000 AU, the outer cloud beyond.
  One figure. Cross-check: Portegies Zwart et al. 2021 (below), sec.
  2.2, "inner edge of the Oort cloud (<~20 000 au)".
- `GRAVITATIONAL_INFLUENCE_AU` (150,000, no source, "visualization
  boundary"): Portegies Zwart, Torres, Cai and Brown 2021, A&A 652,
  A144, sec. 2.3 and the captions of Figs. 3 and 4 -- the Sun's Hill
  radius in the Galactic potential, about 0.65 pc, from a full Galaxy
  model; the outer limit of the Oort cloud taken to coincide with it
  (Chebotarev 1965). Store `GRAVITATIONAL_INFLUENCE_PC` = 0.65, 2
  figures; `_AU` derived (about 134,000). The old
  `GRAVITATIONAL_INFLUENCE_RANGE_AU` (100,000-200,000, unsourced) and
  the orrery hover that prints it go. TRAP: Fig. 4 also gives an inner
  edge of 0.21 pc -- a different definition (where the tide dominates
  in their model), not NASA's inner edge; do not mix them.
- `ALFVEN_SURFACE_RADII` (19.7): Kasper et al. 2021, PRL 127, 255101,
  text -- the probe moved from 19.7 to 18.4 solar radii. 3 figures.
- `HELMET_CUSP_RADII` (4.0): the row quotes Suess and Nerney 2004 --
  helmets "reach no higher than 2-4 solar radii" -- read at L-224; not
  reopened (not found online today). Top of a 2-4 range: 1 figure.
- `INNER_CORONA_RADII` (3): already a declared convention (Lamy);
  needs a print count.
- `OUTER_CORONA_RADII` (50): Mann et al. 2004 not found; the row calls
  it a visualization boundary -- declared drawing choice.
- `ROCHE_LIMIT_RADII` (3.45): derived from a comet density of "~500",
  one figure -- prints as 3 solar radii.
