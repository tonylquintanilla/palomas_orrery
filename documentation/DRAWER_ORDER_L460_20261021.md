# The drawer order, by where each shell begins (L-460)

Built on gallery b334077 at
https://github.com/tonylquintanilla/tonyquintanilla.github.io and orrery
c0feb22 at https://github.com/tonylquintanilla/palomas_orrery. Written
October 21, 2026 with Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5.

Your ruling, 2026-10-20: each shell takes its place in the drawer at the
distance where it begins. The website now reads that distance from each
shell's served row when the page opens, so a new shell lands in place by
itself. This is the order it produces. The Solar System room is not
changed: its rows are bodies, outward from the Sun.

How "where it begins" is read, in order:
- A shell with an inner edge on its row begins there.
- A shell drawn as stored dots begins at its nearest dot.
- The magnetopause and the bow shock begin at their nose.
- A sphere or a ring begins at the distance it is drawn at.
- Rows that are not shells keep their places: the Sun, the Moon, the
  galactic plane, the axis, the Sun direction, the terminator.
- Two shells that begin at the same distance keep their present order.

Three rows are marked **LOOK**. Each is where the rule gives an answer
you may not expect.
