## Protocol v3.38 and the gate amendment

Two changes. The two dead `documentation/PROJECT_ORIGIN.md` pointers were
corrected (the file is at the repo root), and **Stale Skill = Stop gained
its two known limits.**

Claude proposed an amendment that would have let a session clear the gate
on Tony's word that he had reinstalled the skill. **Tony declined it**, on
the grounds that an assertion is not a verification and accepting one in
place of a read is cite-to-clear moved into the skill layer. He also
noted the architectural fact underneath: the only true verification is
the updated skill visible to Claude at the next session.

What landed instead records the two limits and adds no escape:

- The gate is **load-triggered**. A manifest that changes later in the
  same session produces a mismatch with nothing left to fire on. That is
  what happened tonight -- the 1.8 load matched a 1.8 manifest correctly,
  and the mismatch surfaced only because a later check re-read the file
  for an unrelated reason.
- A **mid-session reinstall cannot be verified from inside the session.**

The verification therefore defers into the handoff, which is why this
document opens with it.
