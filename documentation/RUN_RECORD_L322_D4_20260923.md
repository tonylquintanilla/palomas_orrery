## Why

- **Tony's ruling, 2026-09-23: "auto scale should show any rendered
  feature."** Auto sized a center body's cube to twice its outermost
  sphere shell only. With Earth's crust alone the cube was 0.000085 AU
  in each direction, while the axis and dipole cone reach about
  0.00013 AU. Plotly draws nothing outside the cube, so both tips, and
  the hover markers on them, were cut off. That is why no hover showed
  until the scale was set by hand.
- **Tony's request: add the live check to the dashboard.**
- **Tony's live run of D3 (2026-09-24) made the old pass test weak.**
  Against the smoothed mean, today's nod used 8.46 of the 10-arcsecond
  allowance. Against ERFA's true obliquity the same run differed by
  0.25 arcseconds at most, on all five dates from 2000 to 2100.

## What the patch changes

Six files, all or nothing.

- `planet_visualization.py`: a new function, `auto_cube_half_width`, the
  one Auto rule: the larger of twice the outermost sphere shell (the old
  size, so nothing gets smaller) and 1.2 times the farthest vertex of
  every feature the dispatch drew for the body. 1.2 is Fly To's margin.
  The dispatch records that reach as `fig._body_feature_extent_au`
  BEFORE it adds the Sun direction arrow, and leaves the arrow out: it
  only points, has a 0.001 AU minimum length and would make Earth a speck
  (0.0012 AU across instead of 0.00016). Under Auto, for the center
  body, the arrow is fitted inside the cube instead.
- `shared_utilities.py`: `create_sun_direction_indicator` takes
  `fit_to_range`. When True the arrow is shortened to fit the cube even
  below its minimum. Default False, so its other callers (comets, the
  asteroid belts, the per-frame tool) are unchanged.
- `palomas_orrery.py`: both branches of `add_center_body_shells`, the
  Sun and the planets, size the Auto cube with the rule above. The
  static plot and the animation both go through this function.
- `earth_pole_live_check.py`: PASS and FAIL against ERFA's true
  obliquity with a 1-arcsecond allowance, four times the largest
  difference Tony's run measured. The mean is still printed, marked
  information only.
- `earth_pole_of_date.py`: Earth's axis hover names the Earth-Moon
  barycenter, the gravitational center of the Earth-Moon system, where it
  said "the Earth-Moon orbit", which could be read as the Moon's orbit
  around Earth, a different plane (Tony's wording, 2026-09-23). One
  docstring paragraph corrected.

## What the Auto cube does now, measured

Every body drawn as the center through the live dispatch, on a copy of
`1319e496` with D4 applied. "All inside" means every vertex of every
trace, the Sun arrow included, lies inside the new cube.

- **Earth, crust only:** 0.000085 AU becomes 0.000159 AU (1.9 times).
  All inside.
- **Earth, magnetosphere, belts, LEO and GEO on:** 0.000085 AU becomes
  0.0145 AU (170 times). The bow shock reaches 284 Earth radii and was
  almost entirely outside before.
- **Sun, core, radiative zone and photosphere:** 0.0093 AU becomes
  0.29 AU (31 times). The Sun's rotation axis is drawn 52 solar radii
  long (`half_len_frac` 50 in `PLANET_ROTATION`), so the inner shells
  become small in the view.
- **Mercury with every shell:** 0.0031 AU becomes 0.027 AU (8.9 times),
  set by the sodium tail at about 1,400 Mercury radii.
- Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Venus, the Moon and Pluto grow
  by 1.2 to 1.5 times with one inner shell, where the axis sets the size,
  and do not change with every shell on.
- Every case: all inside.
