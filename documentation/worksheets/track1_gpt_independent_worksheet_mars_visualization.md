## Disposition summary

The Mars calibration pass does not support a blanket “all four claims match” result. Claim group 2 converges cleanly. Claim group 3 converges on the actual model constant `1.64 R_M`, while the explanatory `1.5 R_M` is merely rounded low. Claim group 1 is mostly supported, but the categorical no-stratosphere statement needs a dedicated source before V2 promotion. Claim group 4 contains a meaningful numerical divergence: approximately 1.1 million km is correct, but the internally consistent JPL-based value is about 320 Mars mean radii, not 324.5, and the perihelion lower bound should be about 0.98 million km rather than 0.8 million km.
