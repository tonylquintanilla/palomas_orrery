Supplementary Note on Secondary Source Errors
When reviewing the literature surrounding Haumea's dimensions, there is a recurring error in widely copied secondary sources regarding the 2017 occultation data. Many databases and articles incorrectly label the Ortiz et al. 2017 semi-axes (1161 \times 852 \times 513 km) as full axial lengths. If treated as full lengths, Haumea's volume-equivalent radius drops to \approx 398 km—an extreme error that fundamentally alters mass and density derivations. If your visualizations map axes dynamically based on public datasets, checking whether the source dataset has mistakenly halved the physical size of the body by treating the Ortiz semi-axes as full diameters is strongly recommended.
