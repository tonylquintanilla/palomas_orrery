## 1. What was built

- **The 15 conversion rows became names, not rows** (`constants_new.py`).
  Each keeps its name, its `# Unit:` line and its formula, and carries
  `# Conversion: of <the row it comes from>` in place of its own figure
  count, status, source and working. Where a formula was a sum written
  in another unit (the stratopause, the thermopause, the chromosphere)
  or went through another conversion (the two standoffs' AU), it now
  divides or multiplies its one source row. Every stored value is
  identical to the last bit, checked name by name before and after.
- **`constants_rows.py`** reads the marker (`Row.conversion_of`), finds
  every row shaped like a conversion (`conversion_shape()`), checks a
  marked one (`conversion_problem()`: one row, scaled only by rows that
  define units, in another unit of the same kind, equal to that row's
  full digits times the factor, with no field of its own), and prints
  one at its source row's count (`conversion_text()`).
- **`export_constants.py`** leaves each conversion out, listed under
  not_exported as "a conversion of X, served as that row's "in"", and
  refuses to write when a marker is wrong. 76 rows are exported, was 91.
- **`test_derived_figures.py`** names any row shaped like a conversion
  that is not marked (UNMARKED CONVERSION: a FAIL inside a closed slice,
  a named gap outside), fails a wrong marker (CONVERSION WRONG), refuses
  a row computed from a conversion, and lists the 15 by name with their
  sources. It also carries provenance-discipline 2.22's widening: a
  single measured row scaled by an exact number keeps its place, and a
  place carried through two scalings is snapped once. Nine new
  fixtures, 54 in all.
- **`test_constants_export.py`**'s closed-slice gate skips a conversion
  only after `conversion_problem()` passes it, and names each one it
  skipped (14 in Earth; the chromosphere is in the Sun's slice).
- **The orrery's crust moved to the mean radius** (`shell_configs.py`):
  `radius_fraction` is `EARTH_MEAN_RADIUS_KM / EARTH_EQUATORIAL_RADIUS_KM`,
  0.99888, computed. The hover and its tooltip twin carry the words Tony
  approved in this session, printing 6,371.000 km by the row's count,
  and credit the NASA fact sheet's mean radius. The frame note and
  `EARTH_MEAN_RADIUS_KM`'s note in `constants_new.py`, and the comment
  beside the crust's `info_polar_deg` (31 km becomes 24.4 km), say so.
- **The chromosphere's orrery hover** prints its radius through
  `conversion_text()`: still "1.003".
- **The master plan is v34** with an executive summary at the top;
  Section 5a gains the 2026-09-23 to 28 subsection; both companion
  files carry a retirement line.
- **Two skill bumps and protocol v3.73** (section 4), and the ledger
  (section 5).

  and separately with the widening switched off, the fixtures failed.
