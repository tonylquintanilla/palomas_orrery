## 3. Where this departs from the manifest, and why

1. **Nine pointer entries, not six, so 87 links, not 84.** Section 5.2
   counted the kilometre, AU and scatter entries for the two standoffs.
   Section 17.4, written later, has the belts print the tilt's rate and
   the outer band's two ends from served rows. Each of those needs its
   own pointer entry of the same pointer-only shape. Fallbacks fall from
   28 to 24, as section 14 says.
2. **The scatter prints 1.23 and 0.69, not "about 1.2".** Section 2.3's
   example predates provenance-discipline 2.17, whose Rule 7 says a
   display prints the declared count and never fewer. Stated in the
   words note Tony approved.
3. **The fixture is named for gallery `42fd97dd`**, the base this was
   built on, not `06fdad8c`. The old fixture, `fixture_hovers_cdfa74c3.json`,
   is left in place and no longer referenced, as section 17.7 records it.
4. **Two approved sentences share a paragraph where they sit together.**
   The kilometre sentence and the scatter sentence run on in one
   paragraph, and so do the tilt sentence and its epoch sentence. The
   words are exactly as approved. Printed on separate lines, the
   magnetopause and the outer belt would each have reached 18 lines,
   past the hover ceiling of 17, which may never be raised.
5. **The tilt entry's own `source` text is not changed.** It still says
   "rounded to a tenth" and "0.05 deg per decade" beside the served
   9.4105. The words tool may edit only entries that carry a display
   name, and this one does not; no hover or panel shows it. Recorded for
   the ledger below.
