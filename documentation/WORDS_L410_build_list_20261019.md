## 3. Two drawing choices the sources do not settle

**3.1 How the dust thins near the Sun.** Lamy and others (2022) give
the two edges: the dust starts to thin at about 19 solar radii and is
gone by about 5. They do not give the shape in between. I drew it as a
straight line: the density is multiplied by a factor that rises evenly
from nothing at 5 solar radii to full at 19. The approved words already
say "thins from about 19 solar radii inward and is gone by about 5", so
no words change. Recommended: the straight line, as the simplest shape
that matches both edges. -- yes. the illustration does not zoom in to see it.  

**3.2 Where the dust cloud is centred.** The COBE model puts the
cloud's centre slightly off the Sun, about 0.013 AU (Kelsall's Table
1). That is about 3 solar radii: invisible at the cloud's own scale,
but a visible shift of the dust-free hole when you zoom in to the
corona. I drew the cloud centred on the Sun and did not say so.
Recommended: keep it centred, because near the Sun the cloud's shape
is warped anyway (Lamy and others), which the words already say the
drawing does not show. -- yes
