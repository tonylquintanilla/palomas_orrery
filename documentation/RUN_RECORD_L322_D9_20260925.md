## 1. Why

Tony's notes on D8, 2026-09-25: from the hover it was unclear whether
the rings are separate features or a way of drawing one region; the
brighter ring was barely brighter; and the gaps between rings were
uneven, which suggests a physical feature rather than a drawing. The
uneven gaps came from D8 itself: the peak ring was added on top of five
evenly spaced rings, so it landed beside one of them.

Neither belt peaks at its middle. The inner belt runs 1.1 to 2.0 Earth
radii and peaks at 1.5; the outer runs 3 to 7 and peaks at 4.5. So an
odd number of evenly spaced rings would put its centre ring at the
middle, not at the peak. Two options were put to Tony; he chose the
second (2026-09-25): "even spacing with enough rings so that the peak
ring can be identified at the correct radius fraction, plus text
explanation."

## 2. What the patch changes

- `earth_visualization_shells.py`
  - New `_even_belt_rings()`: the rings are evenly spaced from the inner
    edge row to the outer edge row, on the largest step that also lands
    exactly on the peak row. The inner belt gets 10 rings, every 0.1
    Earth radii, with the peak the fifth from the inside. The outer belt
    gets 9, every 0.5, with the peak the fourth. The ring count follows
    from the rows; if a row were ever typed so the step became tiny, the
    function refuses above 25 rings rather than drawing hundreds.
  - The peak ring is fully opaque (it was 0.6) and its points are twice
    the size (3.0 against 1.5). The other rings are as before.
  - 48 points a ring, where D8 had 80 with 6 rings, so the belts stay
    about the same size per animation frame.
  - The info marker sits on the peak ring.
  - Both belt hovers say the belt is one continuous region and the rings
    only mark its extent (section 4).
- `measure_perframe_elements.py`: its reduced variant patched two
  literals, `n_points = 80` and `n_rings = 5`. The ring count is no longer
  a literal, so it now halves the points a ring, 48 to 24, and its row
  labels say so.
- A new file, this run record, in `documentation/`.

What moves on screen: the belts' rings, and the belts' cross markers.
Nothing else.

- The drawn traces: the inner belt 480 points, the outer 432; two point
  sizes and two brightnesses in each; each cross marker at the peak's

- The longest rendered hover line is 90 characters.
- The provenance scanner, compared as counted lists of every finding by

  A first draft typed the belt rows' values into the new function's

## 4. The visitor-facing text, old and new

Inner belt, old:

    The rings run across the belt from edge to edge. The brighter ring is the
    flux peak, 1.5 Earth radii from Earth's centre, about

Inner belt, new:

    The belt is one continuous region. The rings only mark its extent: they
    are evenly spaced from its inner edge to its outer edge, and the brighter
    ring is the flux peak, 1.5 Earth radii from Earth's centre, about

The outer belt's hover changes the same way, ending "ring is the flux
peak, L = 4.5 -- about 22,000 km above the". The rest of both hovers is
unchanged.
