## What the patch changes

Five files, thirteen edits, all or nothing.

- `constants_new.py`: seven rows and the pole dict.
  - `S_PER_HOUR` and `ARCSEC_PER_DEG`, exact conversions, sourced to NIST
    SP 811 section 5.1.1 Table 6, opened 2026-09-23.
  - `EARTH_SIDEREAL_ROTATION_PERIOD_H`, derived from
    `EARTH_ROTATION_RATE_RAD_S` through `S_PER_HOUR`: 23.93447 hours at
    seven figures.
  - `EARTH_POLE_RA_J2000_DEG` and `EARTH_POLE_DEC_J2000_DEG`, the frame's
    own axis, drawn only when the pole of the scene's date cannot be
    fetched. The offset between that axis and Earth's mean pole of 2000
    (about 17 and 5 milliarcseconds) is stated from IERS TN36 chapter 2,
    section 2.1.1, opened 2026-09-23.
  - `EARTH_OBLIQUITY_J2000_ARCSEC` = 84381.448 and
    `EARTH_OBLIQUITY_J2000_DEG` computed from it. Sourced to the Horizons
    manual, opened 2026-09-23. Its row says in words that it is the
    frame's angle, not Earth's tilt, and that TN36 Table 1.1 gives the
    IAU 2006 tilt as 84381.406.
  - `planet_poles`, moved from `idealized_orbits.py`. Values unchanged.
    The false "cross-check exactly" sentence is removed; the Moon's entry
    says its cited table row now reads "See Sect. 3".
- `constants_tokens.py`: `arcsec`, `arcsec_per_deg`, `hours`, `s_per_h`.
- `idealized_orbits.py`: imports `planet_poles`; the rotation into the
  ecliptic frame reads `EARTH_OBLIQUITY_J2000_DEG` instead of a typed
  23.439291 labelled "IAU 2006".
- `solar_visualization_shells.py`: one comment names the dict's new home.
- `planet_visualization_utilities.py`: Earth's axis hover.
