## LESSONS WORTH CARRYING

- **A patch may not leave its own output untrue.** The Correction Does
  Not Travel says to ask what QUOTES a value when it changes. Claude
  checked what INTERPOLATED it and stopped there, having written the
  store Note that retired those very claims two hours earlier.
- **A Note is not a store, and it can drift from the Source above it.**
  The outer belt's Source cites Baker at 3 to 6.5 R_E; its Note says
  L = 3 to 7. Same row, no Source stating it, and the gallery repeats
  the Note word for word.
- **Read the whole store before reporting an absence.** Twice this
  session Claude called something missing after searching one place:
  `provenance_exceptions.json` (it is in `data/`) and the gallery's
  belt entries (`van_allen_belts` is its own feature, not under
  `earth_magnetosphere`). Fable caught both.
- **A pipe eats the exit code.** `python3 tool.py | tail -18; echo $?`
  reports `tail`'s status. Claude read a green that was measuring
  nothing and told Tony a failing checker had passed. A Check That
  Cannot Fail, in the smallest possible form.
- **Units convert; states do not.** Tony's ruling. The frame a figure is
  stated in is a convention governed by significant figures. What the
  viewer must be told is whether a number is measured, an envelope, or a
  stylization.
