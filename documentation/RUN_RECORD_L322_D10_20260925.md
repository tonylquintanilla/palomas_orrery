## 1. Why

Tony, 2026-09-25, comparing the gallery's Earth room with the orrery's
Earth plot: the gallery names its grid spacing ("0.0002 AU"), and that
"helps greatly in orientation especially in close views." He asked for
the same in the orrery. He chose the label alone, not the gallery's
turning triad of arrows ("confirmed as recommended"): the orrery's
"Ecliptic Coordinates" box already says which axis is which, and the
triad would be a much larger job.

## 2. What the patch changes

One file, `save_utils.py`, whose `_write_html` writes every orrery plot
to its HTML page.

- New `_inject_grid_label()`, called from `_write_html` after the two
  page scripts it already adds (the encyclopedia and the camera
  tracking). It adds a small box, "grid 0.0002 AU (29,920 km)", in the
  lower left of the page.
- The script reads the spacing Plotly actually drew, from the page's own
  layout after every redraw. So it is right under Auto scale, where
  Python sets no spacing and Plotly picks its own; after a Fly To button,
  which changes the scale after the plot is made; and during camera
  tracking in an animation, which changes it every frame. It only reads
  the layout and writes the box; it cannot change the plot.
- Below a thousandth of an AU the box also gives kilometres, from
  `KM_PER_AU` in `constants_new.py`, as the gallery does.
- If the three axes ever drew different spacings, it names each.
- It appears only on a 3D scene whose axes are titled in AU, which is
  every orrery scene. Star maps in light-years and 2D charts that use the
  same writer get no label rather than a label in the wrong unit. It
  hides when the grid is hidden.
- Where it sits (21 percent from the left, 14 pixels from the bottom,
  below the scene's left edge) is a rendering setting, for Tony's eye.

Nothing on the plot itself moves.

## 3. How it was verified, in the sandbox

Five test pages written through the real `_write_html`, opened in a
headless Chromium with WebGL:

- A close manual scale, half-width 0.001 AU: "grid 0.0002 AU (29,920
  km)". After a relayout like a Fly To button, to a 0.02 AU grid: "grid
  0.02 AU". After a camera zoom: unchanged, as it should be, since
  zooming the camera does not change the grid.
- Auto scale, where Plotly chose the spacing: "grid 0.0005 AU (74,799
  km)", matching the 0.0005 Plotly reported on all three axes.
- A scene titled in light-years: no label.
- A scene with the grid hidden: no label.
- A 40 AU scale: "grid 10 AU".
- An animation whose frames change the scale from a 0.2 to a 0.002 AU
  grid: the label followed, ending at "grid 0.002 AU".
- No script errors on any page.

The agentic pre-test: `save_utils.py` and `palomas_orrery.py` compile,
and the orrery starts headless on a throwaway copy with no errors. The
provenance scanner, compared as counted lists of every finding: 1083
before and after, none gained, none lost.

What could not be tested here: a real orrery plot, which needs Horizons.
