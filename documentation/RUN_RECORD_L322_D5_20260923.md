## What the patch changes

`planet_visualization_utilities.py` only, seven edits.

- **Earth's tilt prints once.** "Dipole tilt: 9.4105 deg from the spin
  axis", from `EARTH_DIPOLE_TILT_DEG` at its declared count. The first
  line had printed "~9.4", the display choosing its own figure count,
  which Rule 7 forbids, beside "9.4105" further down. The note below
  now carries only the epoch and the yearly rate.
- **The typed centre offset is removed** (A Drawing Approximation Does
  Not Promote: source, draw the sourced range, or remove and say so).
  - The offset fraction 0.085, the 540 km and "22 N, 140 E" had no row
    in `constants_new.py` and no source read.
  - The drawing was also wrong in shape. It moved the cone's apex 0.085
    Earth radii north along the spin axis, the whole offset, while the
    same note said the offset points mostly sideways.
  - Sourcing was tried. Koochak and Fraser-Smith (2017), Earth and Space
    Science 4, 626, doi:10.1002/2017EA000280, computes Earth's eccentric
    dipole from IGRF coefficients. The publisher's site refused this
    session twice as a bot, so it was not read, and nothing from it is
    used. The gap goes on L-322.
  - The apex is now at Earth's centre. The hover says: "The magnetic
    centre is not exactly at Earth's centre. That offset is not drawn,
    because its size and direction are not yet sourced here."
- **Plain words in every cone hover.** "Drawn axis is ONE arbitrary
  instant; the cone is the honest sweep" becomes "The line shows the
  magnetic axis at one moment; the cone shows every direction it points
  during one turn". "R_E" goes with the offset line.
- **Every cone hover wraps lines longer than 70 characters** at a space.

## Earth's cone hover, after (line breaks as drawn)

    Earth -- Magnetic Dipole Cone
    Dipole tilt: 9.4105 deg from the spin axis
    Swept about the spin axis once per rotation (sense: prograde)
    The line shows the magnetic axis at one moment; the cone shows every
    direction it points during one turn
    The magnetic centre is not exactly at Earth's centre. That offset is
    not drawn, because its size and direction are not yet sourced here.
    The tilt is for epoch 2020.0 (IGRF-13) and is decreasing by about
    0.0493 deg a year
    Source: Alken et al. 2021, IGRF-13 (Earth Planets Space 73, 49)

Longest line per body, before and after: Mercury 162 to 69, Earth 167 to
68, Jupiter 151 to 69, Saturn 159 to 69, Uranus and Neptune 68.
