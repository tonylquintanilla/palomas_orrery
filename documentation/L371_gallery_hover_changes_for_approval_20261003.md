# The website's Sun cards: what patch_L371_3 changes

Built on gallery d4b408e60b1d9a45252174ca4a2c861ca17b49a5 at
https://github.com/tonylquintanilla/tonyquintanilla.github.io, and orrery
a841ab6ee36fbbcef936408bc87774bc32a7598d at
https://github.com/tonylquintanilla/palomas_orrery plus
patch_L371_2. Written October 3, 2026 with Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5.

These are the cards a visitor opens in the Sun room (?exhibit=sun). The
lines were built here exactly as the website builds them, before and
after, so this is the text the phone will show. Fourteen cards change;
no other card in any room changes.

Every number below is printed from its row at the number of figures its
source supports. None is typed into the words, including the numbers in
your notes.

Please answer "approved", or name the lines you want changed. Most of
this you have already approved in the orrery's file; the three things
that are new are marked NEW.

---

## The far shells now say their radius in AU

NEW: until now these cards gave only "= km (AU)", and the AU was
sometimes printed like "2.00e+3". Now each says its radius in AU first,
then its kilometres.

**Termination Shock**
Old: `= 14,062,199,846 km (94.0 AU)`
New: `Radius: 94.01 AU` / `= 14,064,000,000 km`

**Heliopause**
Old: `Radius: 26148 solar radii` / `= 18,191,163,600 km (122 AU)`
New: `Radius: 121 AU` / `= 18,100,000,000 km`

**Inner Limit of Oort Cloud**
Old: `= 299,195,741,400 km (2.00e+3 AU)`
New:
> Radius: 2,000 AU
> = 300,000,000,000 km
> Thought to lie between 2,000 and 5,000 AU; drawn at 2,000.

**Inner Oort Cloud**
Old: `= 2,991,957,414,000 km (2.00e+4 AU)`
New: `Radius: 20,000 AU` / `= 3,000,000,000,000 km`

**Outer Oort Cloud**
Old: `= 14,959,787,070,000 km (1.00e+5 AU)`
New:
> Radius: 100,000 AU
> = 10,000,000,000,000 km
> Thought to lie between 10,000 and 100,000 AU; drawn at 100,000.

The kilometres are at one figure, as you confirmed: about 15 trillion
prints as 10 trillion.

NEW: its description changes "about a light-year and a half from the
Sun" to "about two light-years from the Sun", to match the one figure
and the orrery hover you approved. The same change is made in the outer
Oort clumps' info panel text.

**Gravitational Influence**
Old: `= 22,439,680,605,000 km (1.50e+5 AU)`
New:
> Radius: 134,000 AU
> = 20,100,000,000,000 km
> The Sun's Hill radius in the galaxy, calculated where the galaxy's
> tidal pull overtakes the Sun's gravity: about 0.65 parsecs.
> Calculated, not measured.

The shell is drawn smaller, at 134,000 AU instead of 150,000.

## The Oort shapes' distance lines

**Hills Cloud (torus)**
Old: `From 2,000 AU (2.99e+11 km) to 20,000 AU (2.99e+12 km)`
New: `From 2,000 AU (300,000,000,000 km) to 20,000 AU (3,000,000,000,000 km)`

**Outer Oort Cloud (clumps)** and **Galactic Tide**, the same line:
Old: `From 20,000 AU (2.99e+12 km) to 100,000 AU (1.50e+13 km)`
New: `From 20,000 AU (3,000,000,000,000 km) to 100,000 AU (10,000,000,000,000 km)`

## The corona

**Inner Corona**
Old: `= 2,087,100 km (0.0140 AU)`
New: `= 2,000,000 km (0.01 AU)`

**Outer Corona**
Old: `= 34,785,000 km (0.233 AU)`
New:
> = 35,000,000 km (0.23 AU)
> A boundary chosen for the drawing; the faint outer corona has no
> sharp edge.

Its info panel no longer shows a Source line: the citation could not be
found saying 50 solar radii, and the value is now declared a drawing
choice.

**Alfven Surface**
Old: `= 13,705,290 km (0.0916 AU)`
New: `= 13,700,000 km (0.092 AU)`

**Streamer Belt**
Old: `Cusp: 4 solar radii` / `= 2,782,800 km (0.0186 AU)`
New:
> Cusp: 4 solar radii
> = 3,000,000 km (0.02 AU)
> Helmets reach no higher than 2 to 4 solar radii; drawn at 4.

and its fade line, as the Alfven surface: `= 13,700,000 km (0.092 AU)`.

## The Roche limit

Old: `Radius: 3.45 solar radii` / `= 2,400,165 km (0.0160 AU)`
New:
> Radius: 3 solar radii
> = 2,000,000 km (0.02 AU)
> Known only roughly, because comets differ in density. It is drawn
> where the formula's middle answer falls; the true limit could lie
> inside or outside the inner corona.

NEW: the third line is new wording, not yet approved. It says in a
visitor's words what we worked out together: the Roche limit is known to
one figure, it is drawn at 3.45 so it doesn't sit on the inner corona,
and we can't tell which is further out.

The shell is still drawn at 3.45 solar radii, outside the inner corona,
as the orrery draws it.

## Info panel text

The termination shock's info panel says Voyager 1 crossed at 94.01 AU
instead of 94 AU. Each Oort edge's Source line now names what was read:
NASA's Oort Cloud facts page for the two outer edges, and Portegies
Zwart et al. (2021) for the 20,000 AU edge and the Hill radius.
