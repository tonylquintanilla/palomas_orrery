## What the tests found (Nov 14; re-check, do not trust)

- Google Earth's phone APP shows a project's top-level items as pages
  ("Table of contents 1/1"). An import always arrives as ONE top-level
  folder, so every import is 1/1, and the app offers a visitor no way
  to switch the folders inside it.
- Google Earth on the WEB, opened in a phone's browser, lets a visitor
  switch folders on and off; the colors pile up unless one is switched
  off.
- What the OWNER switches on or off is saved as what visitors see.
  Tony browsing his own project changes the public view.
- The website's button links to the right project (1tvCdw...); the old
  June project is deleted.

## The design

THE FORM. The page `earth/food_insecurity_sdn.html` becomes a short
scroll of steps, each about one phone screen: a picture on top, then
two or three sentences. Plain scrolling, no buttons to learn, upright
phone first. The generator writes the page and every picture at each
build (as today), into the orrery's data/earth/ and copied to the
gallery's earth/. Pictures are named `earth/<layer id>_<step>.png`
(e.g. `food_insecurity_sdn_lean.png`); each has alt text saying what it
shows.

THE STEPS, in season order (ruling 6):
1. What this map shows. IPC's five phases, each with its color named in
   words, and the 20-percent rule, before any map. Picture: the five
   phase colors as a key.
2. February - May 2026. Picture: the whole country, Current period.
   Words: IPC's national figures (the generator's existing constants).
3. June - September 2026, the lean season. Picture: Projected 1, the 56
   areas, other areas white. Words: IPC's figures (PDF page 22); the
   coverage line; IPC's caution about comparing periods (page 1); the
   share in Crisis or worse rising "from 60 percent to 67 percent"
   (page 30).
4. October 2026 - January 2027, the harvest, now. Picture: Projected 2.
   Words: IPC's figures (page 28); the share expected "to decline to 59
   percent" (page 30).
5. Hidden under the colors. Picture: a close-up of Darfur and Kordofan,
   Projected 2, with the Catastrophe dots. Words: the 20-percent rule
   once more, plainly, and IPC's "over 71,000 people in Catastrophe
   (IPC Phase 5)" (page 28).
6. Risk of Famine. Picture: Projected 2 with the symbol (after IPC) on
   each area IPC names. Words: IPC's meaning, a reasonable worst-case
   scenario, not a declaration (page 26); the count of areas as IPC
   states it.
7. About this map. From IPC / what this map adds / what it does not
   show / IPC's own map as the authority (today's section), then the
   button: "Explore the map in Google Earth", with one line saying it
   opens on October 2026 - January 2027 and that in a web browser the
   list on the left switches periods. Promise nothing else.

RESPECTFUL AWARENESS (ruling 4) governs every word: IPC's figures and
phrasing, quoted with page; no adjectives of our own on suffering; no
causes we do not source (earth-system-pipeline, the restraint
discipline); people stated plainly as people. American spelling in
composed sentences; IPC's quotations keep IPC's.

THE MAP FILE'S WORDS. The "i" pop-up and the period folders'
descriptions say what Google Earth does: the map opens on October 2026
- January 2027; in a web browser the list switches periods; nothing
about switching in the phone app. Rebuild the KMZ.
