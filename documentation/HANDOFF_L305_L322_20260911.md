1. **L-305 Gap items 1 and 2 closed by reading.** Both papers read from
   Tony's uploaded PDFs. Shue's eqs. 10-11 and Table 1 confirmed with
   their Monte Carlo standard deviations; Jelinek's six values confirmed
   at eqs. 13-16 and sec. 4. The tail extent sourced to Ness et al.
   (1967) on the abstract route, and the figure changed from "past
   1,000 R_E" to "to about 1,000 R_E" with its qualifier. Landed by
   `patch_L305_paper_read.py`, pushed at `2432db64`.
2. **Every computed figure in L-305 recomputed from the published
   equations** rather than from the block: 10.25, 0.59, 28.9, 13.51,
   11.24, the 105 degree cut at -7.8 and 29.0, and 67 and 46 at
   x = -100. All reproduce. That is the independent check L-305 recorded
   as still missing.
3. **Four corrections to the record**, each in the patch: Shue is in
   aberrated GSM, not GSE (it changes nothing drawn, and the record says
   why so nobody "fixes" it); Shue's validity range is readable in the
   1998 paper so Shue 1997 is not needed; 2 nPa is the paper's own
   average while Bz = 0 and 400 km/s are not; Jelinek's eqs. 17-18 are a
   validation refit, not the model.
4. **The helium factor, read from the caption Tony photographed.**
   Dp includes helium by (1 + 0.04 N_alpha) with N_alpha a PERCENTAGE.
   It was left out of the first patch because the scan was illegible,
   and folded in once Tony sent the page. The percentage-versus-fraction
   distinction is the one that would have bitten L-314: 0.04 instead of
   4 gives 1.0016 rather than 1.16, a 14% error in dynamic pressure.
5. **Gap item 3 read, and it opened an architecture.** Full account in
   L-322 and the design record. Short version: the drift check reads
   units from name suffixes, L-305's twelve non-length values would
   report NO UNIT, and NO UNIT does not fail. A patch extending the
   suffix table was written, tested green, and HELD UNRUN because it put
   a naming vocabulary for orrery constants in the gallery repo.
6. **Nine rulings, one Fable review round.** All nine held. The review
   caught a two-hop error in the record and contributed the whole-store
   export and the one-walk migration. Two of its own numbers were wrong
   and are corrected in revision 2.

## LESSONS WORTH CARRYING

- **A read is Claude's job, not a desk task.** Twice this session Tony
  was handed a "read the code at your desk" step for code that is
  public and fetchable. He named it both times. If the artifact is in a
  repo, the read happens in session.
- **The measurement changed the design twice.** The prefix bound
  (`SUN_`/`EARTH_`) was rejected because four Sun constants carry no
  prefix. The marker sizes were re-homed because `palomas_orrery.py`
  imports FROM helpers, so helpers cannot import back. Neither was
  visible from reasoning; both took one command.
- **A held patch is a deliverable.** `patch_L305_store_drift_units.py`
  is correct, tested, and must not run. Writing it was how its own
  wrongness became visible -- it works, and the naming constraint it
  would impose on orrery constants from inside the gallery repo is the
  tell.
- **Tony's engineering default beat the code's convention.** "In
  engineering I have always used units, not literals" is ruling 1, and
  the gallery's own comment already said the same thing and had been
  read past for weeks.
