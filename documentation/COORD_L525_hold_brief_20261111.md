## What this is, in one paragraph

On Nov 11 one wrong test result for Halley cut the whole site's trusted
dates to 0.3 days, froze every room and turned the nights red. The
builder's rule is that the shortest trusted span among the bodies sets
the span for everything (L-149). Tony ruled that a body which fails its
accuracy test is held and reported, and the rest of the site carries
on. This session builds that: the hold in the nightly builder, the
report where Tony will see it without looking, and the sentence a
